## Competitive Advantages

1. **Real-time Competition** - No other platform offers live classroom math battles
2. **Procedural Questions** - Unlimited unique questions, no repetition
3. **CCSS Alignment** - Every question mapped to standards
4. **Teacher Control** - Difficulty, timing, all in teacher's hands
5. **Low Barrier** - Free tier is genuinely useful, not crippled

---

## Risk Mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Low sponsor interest | Start with education-focused companies, prove ROI |
| Merchandise inventory | Use print-on-demand, zero inventory risk |
| District sales cycle (long) | Focus on individual teachers first, they become advocates |
| Competition from big players | Move fast, build community, focus on real-time niche |
